Nekoverse - GameFI

  • 2022 — 2023

    Nekoverse is a Play-to-Earn MMORPG built on blockchain technology, offering players a decentralized in-game economy and a Guild system utilizing DAO governance. Players can engage in PvE and PvP battles, explore the world, farm resources, and craft items.

  • Key Features:

    • ⚔️ Real-time Skill-based Battles: Engage in various modes like 1v1, 3v3, Tournaments, and Boss Raids, utilizing elements, skills, and equipment.

    • 🌍 Exploration & Resource Farming: Discover hidden areas, harvest resources like ores and wood, and sell them in the marketplace.

    • 🛠️ Comprehensive Crafting System: Craft items that drive game progression and impact battle prowess.

    • 🏛️ Neko Temple: Stake in-game currencies to earn governance tokens and passive income, enhancing community engagement.

  • My Role:

    • 👨‍💻 Served as a Senior Unity Developer, collaborating with a team to implement core gameplay mechanics and optimize performance.

    • 🔧 Developed real-time battle systems, including skill mechanics and elemental interactions.

    • 🌐 Integrated blockchain features, such as NFT assets and staking mechanisms, into the Unity environment.

    • 📊 Conducted performance profiling and optimization to ensure smooth gameplay on various devices.

  • Production Highlights:

    • Fundraising impact: Nekoverse successfully raised million-dollar funding as a GameFi MMORPG product.

    • GameFi economy surface: gameplay systems were designed around NFT ownership, staking, resource farming, crafting, and marketplace-driven value loops.

    • Battle system complexity: implemented real-time PvE/PvP logic with elemental interactions, skill execution, equipment modifiers, and combat feedback.

    • Live product constraints: collaborated with a production team where gameplay, backend, blockchain, UI, and economy decisions had to stay aligned.

    • Performance ownership: profiled gameplay scenes and optimized device-facing bottlenecks for smoother mobile play.

  • Technical Challenges Solved:

    • Connected blockchain-facing ownership concepts to Unity gameplay without making the player flow feel like a wallet-first application.

    • Kept skill, equipment, and elemental combat rules extensible so balance changes could be made without rewriting battle logic.

    • Supported a mixed PvE/PvP feature set where responsiveness, clarity, and backend integration all affected the final player experience.
