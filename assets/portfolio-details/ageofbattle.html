Age of Battle

  • Key Features:

    • 🏰 5 Historical Stages: Progress through Prehistoric, Ancient, Classical, Renaissance, and Modern eras with unique units and mechanics.

    • ⚔️ Strategic Unit Deployment: Spawn Melee & Ranged units with configurable stats. Each unit type has unique attack patterns and behaviors.

    • 🌊 Wave System: Battle through enemy waves with ScriptableObject-driven configurations. Difficulty scales with stage progression.

    • 🃏 Card Collection System: Earn cards across 4 rarity tiers (Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary) with 15+ stat types for permanent army bonuses.

    • 📈 Progression Systems: Upgrade units, stronghold health, food production, and unlock evolve tiers for permanent stat multipliers.

    • 💎 Economy & Monetization: Earn coins in-battle, manage gems as premium currency, with IAP and AdMob integration.

    • 🎯 Daily Tasks: Rotating daily objectives rewarding gems and card packs to keep players engaged.

    • ⚡ PvP Mode: Dedicated player-vs-player battles for competitive gameplay.

  • Technical Highlights:

    • 🏗️ Architecture: Modular design with GameManager state machine, DataManager for persistence, UIManager for view orchestration.

    • 📊 Data-Driven: All gameplay data driven by ScriptableObjects (Stages, Units, Waves, Cards, Tasks, Food Production).

    • 🎮 Performance: Object pooling for units and VFX to minimize GC, optimized for mobile performance.

    • 🎨 Visual Effects: Blood splatter, cannon explosions, hit SFX per unit type. DOTween for smooth UI/game object animations.

    • 📱 Cross-Platform: Android, iOS, and WebGL support. Haptic feedback via NiceVibrations on mobile.

    • 💰 Monetization: Google AdMob + Unity Ads (Banner, Interstitial, Rewarded), Unity IAP for in-app purchases.

  • Game Flow:

    • 1️⃣ Earn Resources: Gain food/coins during battle by dealing damage to the enemy tower.

    • 2️⃣ Deploy Units: Spend resources to spawn your army. Choose unit types strategically based on available roster.

    • 3️⃣ Execute Strategy: Units automatically march toward the enemy base and engage enemies. Melee units clash while ranged units fire projectiles.

    • 4️⃣ Survive & Conquer: Survive all enemy waves → Destroy the enemy stronghold → Victory!
