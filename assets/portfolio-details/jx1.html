Jx Mobie(JX1)

  • 2023 - 2024

    Jx Mobie is ADNX's mobile MMORPG built around classic Vietnamese martial-arts MMO expectations: faction identity, class progression, open-world activities, and large-scale PvP.

    The official product supports smartphones, tablets, and PC, with a class system spanning multiple sects and signature features such as Phong Lang Do and Tong Kim battlefield content.

  • My Role:

    • Worked as a Senior Unity Developer on a publisher-backed MMORPG production at ADNX Games.

    • Contributed to gameplay implementation, mobile optimization, resource loading, SDK integration, and multiplayer feature delivery.

    • Supported systems needed for faction-based progression and large-scale PvP content across mobile targets.

  • Key Features:

    • 10 martial sects: sect identities such as Con Lon, Vo Dang, Nga My, Thuy Yen, Cai Bang, Thien Nhan, Ngu Doc, Duong Mon, Thien Vuong, and Thieu Lam.

    • Large-scale multiplayer content: official feature areas include Phong Lang Do and Tong Kim battlefield modes.

    • Cross-device delivery: official support spans smartphone, tablet, and PC, with mobile requirements starting from Android 7.1 / iOS 12.

  • Production Highlights:

    • Publisher MMORPG scope: contributed to a culturally recognizable martial-arts MMO product under ADNX Games.

    • Multiplayer readiness: worked on systems and optimization concerns required for high-concurrency PvP experiences.

    • Mobile production: supported loading, rendering, SDK, and platform-specific concerns needed for broad device coverage.
